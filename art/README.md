# Card art (work in progress)

This folder holds source art for four alternate Cuttle themes. **None of it is
wired into the game yet.** It lives here as review material, not as a
`web/static/themes/` asset set — that move happens after review, revision,
and cropping (see "Next steps" below).

## The themes

- **Mythic** (`mythic/`) — painted cards, colour by rank rather than by suit.
  Every card of a given rank shares one palette and one spell or role (Aces
  are Wrath of God in white and pale gold, 2s are Counterspell in azure and
  black, and so on); the suit shows up only in the corner index, stamped in
  code, never in the art itself. Ranks mix stained glass, woodcut and
  painterly treatments freely. The 8s are a second pass (`eights/`, built
  around a pair of goggles — see "Known issues"). Four `glasses-<suit>.webp`
  files are the Telepathy enchantment art paired with the 8s.
- **Stained glass** (`stained-glass/`) — a hand-written SVG set: real vector
  leaded-glass windows, generated by a small script (polar pane geometry,
  seeded pane colours), not an image model. Only six cards exist today (the
  four Aces plus 2 of Spades and King of Hearts) as a proof of style.
- **Cathedral** (`cathedral/`) — a palette-knife impasto style built from a
  photo of the owner's own painting, used as a style-and-technique reference
  for an image model (not copied for composition). Architecture carries the
  card's meaning instead of a figure: the Ace is a blazing rose window at
  the end of a nave, the King is a crowned apse, sevens are seven arches.
  One card back and one playmat round out the set.
- **Webb** (`webb/`) — playmat and card-back art built from a real NASA/ESA/
  CSA James Webb Space Telescope image of IC 348 (`backs/back-RB*.webp`,
  `mats/mat-R*.webp`), plus four generated space-photograph alternates for
  the mats (`mat-A` through `mat-D`) and three generated alternates for the
  backs (`back-CB*.webp`). See `CREDITS.md` for the required NASA credit
  line on the real-photo cards.

## Known issues

- The Mythic and Cathedral card faces are rendered at a 2:3 ratio; the game
  needs 1:1.3. They all need a crop pass before export.
- The Mythic 8s are a second version (`eights/`, "v2"), built around a pair
  of goggles that reads as an "8" — the first pass is not included here.
- The four `glasses-<suit>.webp` files are rendered landscape (goggles seen
  face-on) and still need the rotate-into-portrait treatment the 8-rank
  cards already got.
