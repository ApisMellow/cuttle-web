# Credits

The Webb theme's real-photo playmats (`webb/mats/mat-R1-webb-star.webp`,
`webb/mats/mat-R2-webb-nebula.webp`) and card backs
(`webb/backs/back-RB1-star.webp`, `webb/backs/back-RB2-bigstar.webp`,
`webb/backs/back-RB3-jets.webp`) are derived from the James Webb Space
Telescope image of IC 348:

**Image credit: NASA, ESA, CSA, Kevin Luhman (PSU), Catarina Alves de Oliveira (ESA), Mahdi Zamani (ESA/Webb)**

Source: https://science.nasa.gov/asset/webb/ic-348-collage/ — the collage
and its crop renditions. The images are cropped, symmetrised, bordered and
tonally adjusted, with a darkened centre band so cards read clearly on top.
They are not endorsed by NASA, ESA or CSA.

The other Webb-theme playmats and card backs (`mat-A` through `mat-D`,
`back-CB1` through `back-CB3`) are original generated renders, not derived
from the NASA/ESA image.

The Cathedral theme's palette-knife impasto style is built from a photo of
ApisMellow's own painting, used only as a technique-and-palette reference
for the image model — no composition was copied from it.
