# Art review sheet

How to review: for each row, mark a **Verdict** of `keep`, `revise`, or
`redo`, and add anything worth carrying forward in **Notes** — what's
wrong, what to try instead, a suit or rank that reads badly at small size,
anything. This sheet is the input to the next art pass: whatever gets
marked `revise` or `redo` is what gets rebuilt, and the notes are the brief
for it.
