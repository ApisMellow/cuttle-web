# Prompt log

Prompts used to generate the art in this folder, merged per theme, model
names kept. File paths below are relative to each theme's own working
folder, not to this repo.

## Mythic

Model throughout: `openai:gpt-image-2`, size 1024x1536 (1536x1024 for the
landscape glasses cards), quality medium. Every prompt asks for "a large
solid pure-white rounded plaque with a thick black outline" in the
upper-left corner — the code-drawn index stamp covers that area afterward,
so the prompt only needs to keep it clear.

## Stained glass

No image model. The geometry is generated by a small Python script (polar
pane math, seeded randomness for pane colours); every shape is a plain
`<path>`, `<circle>` or gradient — no raster, no fonts.

Six cards exist: four Aces (Wrath of God — a 16-ray sun disc over broken
ash-grey ground; a widening pillar of light hitting a golden crater with a
white starburst; a 12-petal rose window; a descending sword of judgement
inside a gold halo), 2 of Spades (Counterspell — the "prohibited" sign in
azure glass, with spell shards bouncing off it), and King of Hearts (a
five-point gold crown with gems and an ermine band, on a violet rose). The
suit appears only in the index; the four Aces are one family with four
different scenes.

Scaling to a full 52-card set: pull the frame, index glyphs and pips into
one shared sprite (`<symbol>` + `<use>`), keep one focal motif per card
(shared per rank for point cards 3–10, so 32 cards need about 8 motifs
rather than 32), and cut the outer pane-ring count. Estimated card size
drops from 21–30 KB to about 12–18 KB.

## Cathedral

Model: `google:gemini-3-pro-image-preview`, high, with a cropped reference
photo of the owner's own palette-knife painting passed as a style-and-
technique reference (composition is not copied from it). Faces returned at
848x1264 and were upscaled to 1024x1536 before the index was stamped.

Architecture carries the card's meaning; there are no figures.

**Ace of Spades**
> Looking down a dark cathedral nave toward a huge circular rose window at the far end that blazes with cool white-gold light. The light floods down the nave in a great wedge, washing the stone floor almost white, bleaching everything it touches. STYLE (technique and palette only, not composition): a thick acrylic impasto painting laid on with a palette knife — distinct flat knife strokes and blocky rectangular slabs of paint with raised ridges that catch the light. - Centre treatment for the playmats: a full-width calm band from 20% to
  74% of the height with cosine falloff — heavily blurred, contrast to
  0.5, saturation to 0.75, brightness auto-scaled so the band's mean
  luminance is about 20/255, so cards read clearly when laid on top.
